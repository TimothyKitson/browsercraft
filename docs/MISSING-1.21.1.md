# What Browsercraft does not have, against Minecraft 1.21.1

Checked on 2026-11-10 against the 1.21.1 registries, data files and decompiled client source. Seven passes read the game's code one area at a time and wrote down what is missing and what is only partly there. A second pass then took every one of those entries and tried to find the thing in the code after all. It found nothing that exists after all, and corrected thirteen entries; the corrections are written on the entries. This replaces the list from early October, which about forty finished to-do items had made wrong.

Names in `code type` are Minecraft's ids. "small / medium / large job" is a rough size: data only, a new shape or a slice of a system, a whole new system.

## The short version

1. **Block shapes.** Most of the 230 missing blocks are ten families: stairs (52), slabs (38), walls (25), wood and hyphae (20), carpets (16), pressure plates (15), mob heads (13), buttons (12), fences (11) and fence gates (11). Stairs, slabs, fences and carpets already have a shape for a few materials, so the rest of those is mostly data. Walls, gates, plates, heads, wall torches and snow layers need a new shape. 225 items are missing, nearly all of them the item forms of those blocks.
2. **How blocks behave.** Nothing falls (sand, gravel, anvils). No waterlogging. Slabs are bottom half only, stairs have no upside-down or corner shapes, logs cannot lie sideways. Redstone is on or off with no signal strength. Leaves do not decay, grass does not spread, snow and ice never form, campfires do not cook.
3. **Mobs.** Seven are missing: donkey, mule, mooshroom, ocelot, skeleton horse, zombie horse and the giant. The 75 that exist mostly share one simple brain, so signature behaviour is absent (endermen do not teleport, spiders do not climb, piglins do not barter). Sheep are white and cannot be shorn. No zombie villager curing. The dragon and wither fights are thin. Mobs are not saved with the world, and LAN guests do not see them.
4. **Things you throw.** Snowballs, eggs and ender pearls exist as items but cannot be thrown.
5. **Rules and commands.** 12 of 42 enchantments, and the place they are stored is full. No game rules at all. 13 of 61 gameplay commands, with cut-down syntax. No attributes, no death messages, no scoreboard, teams, titles or data packs. You keep your items when you die.

## World generation

World generation is broad but shallow: almost every structure has a stand-in, yet none is built from Minecraft's pieces. Of the 34 structure ids, 1 is fully present (buried treasure), 30 are partial (hand-coded boxes: one tower for an end city with no ship, one courtyard for a bastion, a 3x3 room grid for strongholds and trial chambers, one corridor for a mineshaft, bone pillars for nether fossils, a generic re-skinned village with 6 building kinds and no bell) and 3 are missing (mesa mineshaft, ocean and nether ruined portals). The root cause is that there is no structure-template/jigsaw system: none of the 1180 vanilla .nbt pieces is used. Of the 191 placed features that biomes list, 61 have a working counterpart, about 100 are partial (ores with flat distributions, 8 one-trunk tree shapes with no big or 2x2 trees, simple coral lumps, single-block dripstone and glowstone, flat sea ice) and 29 are missing outright: lava lakes and all springs, dungeons, fossils, sculk in the deep dark, underwater magma, nether fire/soul fire/mushrooms/blobs, extra ore placements. Terrain is a 2D heightmap in a 128-block-tall world (y 0-127, not -64 to 319), with one tunnel-plus-cavern cave system that never opens to the surface and has no ravines, aquifers, noodle caves or large ore veins. All 64 biome ids do generate, the Nether has its 5 biomes with a lava sea and the End has its island, pillars, dragon, gateways and outer islands, but small end islands, the arrival platform and tall pillars are absent. World types Default, Superflat (9 presets), Large Biomes, Amplified and Single Biome exist; Debug is missing and superflat worlds get no structures. Extras: bonus chest and three explorer maps exist; /locate, the buried-treasure map and the 1.21 village/jungle/swamp maps do not, the fortress has no chests, and chest loot uses 16 hand-written tables instead of the 61 vanilla ones.
