  LIVE      browsercraft.net, built 2026-10-03 or later from source that
            only exists on C:\Users\TempAdmin\VoxelEngine. Has the Nether,

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
HONEST SCOPE NOTE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

"1.21 Minecraft" is not a finish line this list reaches. Minecraft is about
fifteen years of work by a full studio. Priorities 1 and 2 are realistic.
Priority 3 is a long but genuine roadmap, and each item is a real feature
that makes the game noticeably more complete. Priority 4 is open-ended by
nature: there are roughly 1000 block types and 80 mobs in 1.21, and adding
them is content work without end rather than a problem to solve.

A better target to aim at: a solid block sandbox with crafting, combat,
mobs, the three dimensions and survival progression. That is Priority 1
through 3, and it would feel like Minecraft to play even though it would
not match it feature for feature.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
LICENSING, BECAUSE IT AFFECTS WHAT CAN SHIP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Whatever ends up in assets/ gets distributed to everyone who loads
browsercraft.net.

  Sounds   The pack supplied is Mojang's own audio: the files carry REAPER
           export tags from Mojang's pipeline, encoder dates spanning 2005
           to 2018, and records/ holds the disc music by C418 and Lena
           Raine. Shipping it is what gets a site taken down. The packer
           tool is neutral and will pack whatever it is given; what goes in
           is the owner's call.

  Textures Primes HD Textures 32x, a third-party pack. No licence file was
           included in the zip. Worth checking its terms before shipping.

  Code     stb_image, stb_image_write and stb_vorbis are public domain.
           SDL2 is zlib-licensed. glm is MIT.
